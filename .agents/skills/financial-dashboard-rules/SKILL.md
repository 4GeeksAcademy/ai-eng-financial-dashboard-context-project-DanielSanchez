# Financial Dashboard Rules

## When to use

Use this skill before changing financial calculations, date or period logic, filters, financial categories, segment handling, comparisons, alerts, or the data contracts used by dashboard metrics.

Keep three kinds of statements distinct:
- **Implemented:** behavior directly confirmed in code or tests.
- **Known divergence:** current code differs from an existing project rule or API description.
- **Pending decision:** product or accounting behavior that has not been specified. Do not choose a policy on the team's behalf.

## Confirmed behavior

### Movements and generated data

- `operation_type` is `income` or `outcome`; categories are `suppliers`, `sales`, `operational`, `administrative`, and `others`; segments are `B2B` and `B2C` (`backend/app/routes.py`, `frontend/src/lib/financial-types.ts`).
- The mock generator creates 30 movements for each of 12 months (360 total), sorts them by date, and routes use seed `42`. Amounts generated by the mock are positive; `operation_type` determines whether they contribute to income or outcome. The Pydantic `amount` field itself has no nonnegative constraint.
- Mock dates use `date.today()`: months earlier than the current month get the current year; the current and later months get the previous year. This yields months across two calendar years. Do not assume the fixed UI label represents that range.

### Calculations and formatting

- `totalIncome` is the sum of `amount` for `income`; `totalOutcome` is the sum for `outcome`.
- `profit = totalIncome - totalOutcome`.
- `profitPercent = profit / totalIncome * 100` when `totalIncome > 0`; otherwise the current implementation returns `0`.
- Monthly points aggregate income and outcome by year and month, then calculate net profit and the same percentage formula.
- The frontend formats currency as `en-US` USD with zero displayed decimal places and percentages with one decimal place. Backend summary amounts are rounded to two decimal places. These are current behaviors, not a complete accounting precision policy.

### Periods, filters, and empty data

- Backend date filters are inclusive: `create_date >= start_date` and `create_date <= end_date`. Available filters include date, category, operation type, and, on relevant endpoints, business type.
- Summary grouping supports day, ISO week, and month. Top categories default to five and accept limits from 1 to 20.
- Comparison uses net values for the requested interval and the immediately preceding interval of equal inclusive duration. `delta_pct` is `null` when the previous net is zero; otherwise its denominator is `abs(previous_net)`.
- Alerts default to a `0.3` threshold, require a positive baseline, and compare each period with the average outcome of earlier periods in the selected summary. The current formula is `(outcome - baseline) / baseline`, and the check is strictly greater than the threshold.
- Empty frontend movement data currently yields zero KPIs, a zero profit percentage, no monthly points, and empty-state messages. Months with no movements are omitted rather than filled with zeroes.
- `build_metrics_facets` currently assumes at least one movement. The mock-backed endpoint supplies movements, but a future empty-capable data source needs an explicit empty-data policy.

## Known divergences and pending decisions

- **Known divergence:** the date rule in `.agents/rules/calendar-and-periods.md` says the displayed period should come from returned movement dates. The current dashboard still uses a fixed `2024 - Full Year` label. Preserve the documented rule; do not treat the fixed label as intended business behavior.
- **Known divergence:** `MetricsAlert.increase_ratio` is calculated as a relative increase, but `frontend/specs/RESPONSE_SCHEMAS.json` describes `outcome_total / baseline_average`. For example, 130 over a baseline of 100 is `0.3` in code and `1.3` under that description. Do not change the calculation, schema, or threshold meaning until the intended definition is decided.
- **Pending:** whether zero income with positive outcomes should produce a `0%`, null, or another margin representation.
- **Pending:** whether missing periods should be omitted, filled with zero, or marked unavailable; how negative amounts, refunds, taxes, and other accounting adjustments should work; the intended currency/precision policy; and the business definitions of categories and B2B/B2C.
- **Pending:** whether the mock's rolling month-to-month year assignment is the intended reporting window, and whether alert thresholds should be strict or inclusive at the boundary.

## Invariants and verification

When changing behavior, preserve or explicitly resolve these invariants:
- Keep income, outcome, profit, and percentage calculations consistent between KPI and monthly aggregation paths.
- Keep calendar dates and inclusive filter boundaries consistent across the backend, frontend, and displayed period; cover month and year boundaries.
- Keep zero-denominator, empty-period, comparison, and alert behavior explicit. Do not silently substitute zero for null or a missing period.
- When changing API fields, categories, or operation types, update backend models, frontend types, and contract tests together.

Add or update focused tests for:
- Known income/outcome totals, net profit, percentage, and the zero-income case.
- Empty input and months with no movements, according to the agreed policy.
- Inclusive date boundaries, a movement on the first day of a month, cross-year grouping, and ISO week-year boundaries.
- Category, operation type, and B2B/B2C filters, including an empty result.
- Equal-length comparison windows, zero previous net, and alert baseline/threshold boundaries, including the resolved `increase_ratio` definition.
- Mock count, chronological order, and reproducibility when changing mock generation.
